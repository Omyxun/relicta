Configure a website

Choose “Listing card” and click a card in the image. Use “Larger element” until its outline surrounds the whole card. Then select each field and click its content. Green outlines show the inferred matches. Only the first page is scanned.

Missing prices count as unpriced. Enable “Allow unpriced” in a job to receive those listings. Keywords use the selected description when available.
